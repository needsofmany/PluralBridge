# Preserve Your Simply Plural Data — Archived

This guide is retained as part of PluralBridge's project history.

The Simply Plural shutdown and emergency preservation period have passed. Simply Plural's servers are no longer available, and the legacy PluralBridge export tooling cannot create a new export.

If you already preserved your Simply Plural data, keep the original export private, backed up, and somewhere under your control.
